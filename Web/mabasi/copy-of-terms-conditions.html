Last update 07.03.2024

This privacy policy describes the way Mabasi Lab uses your data when visiting our website. This data will be used to improve our services and the quality of our website’s content.

At Mabasi Lab we value the privacy of our customers and we believe that tracking your activity such as the websites you have visited, the people you communicate with and what you buy online is a violation of your private sphere. Therefore we will not collect such informations.

We will not subscribe you to our newsletter without personally asking you first. We will not sell any personal informations to any third party business, service provider, other users or affiliates. We will not disclose any of your personal information on the public space.

Collected data

We only collect the following data:

Visitors will be referenced as numbers only, we will not be able to see your name or any other personal informations regarding your private life. At Mabasi we love people and we want you to feel safe and not stalked when visiting our site.

We will retain Your data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use Your Personal Data to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our legal agreements and policies.

Security of Your Personal Data

The security of Your Personal Data is important to Us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect Your Personal Data, We cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If You are a parent or guardian and You are aware that Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us.

Our Service may contain links to other websites that are not operated by Us. If You click on a third party link, You will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise You to review the Privacy Policy of every site You visit.
