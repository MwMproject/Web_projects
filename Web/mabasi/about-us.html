HISTORY

Established in March 2004, in Lausanne Switzerland, Mabasi originaly was designing and producing skateboard Swiss made accessories.

In April 2006, Mabasi took over a wellknown screenprinting workshop located in Champagne (CH). In October 2019, Mabasi again took a turn, by introducing Pad-printing and circular printing machinery.

We take great pride in supplying short terms delivery production, with high end quality and durable prints…and locally printed!

In 2024, we’ve celebrated our 20 years in this business! Taking this opportunity, we are stoked to present you our new company identity!

We are looking forward to the next 20 years together!

Mabasi is your one stop shop for all print needs, from screen printing to embroidery our 20 years of experience will bring all your projects to life.
